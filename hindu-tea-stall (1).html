Our Story

A neighbourhood counter for chai, snacks & everyday needs

Tucked right next to the MDO office in Vatsavai, vikram Tea Stall is the kind of place where regulars don't need to order — the tea is already on its way. It's a simple combination that works: hot tea and snacks for anyone passing through, and everyday kirana essentials so a quick tea break can also mean picking up what's needed at home.

  • Fresh tea brewed through the day, never left sitting
  • Hot snacks made to order alongside your chai
  • Daily kirana essentials — no need for a separate trip
  • Easy to find, right next to the MDO office
